# Privacy Policy — YouTube Shorts Blocker

_Last updated: 2026-07-03_

**YouTube Shorts Blocker does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal
data.** It has no account system, no analytics, no tracking, and no remote
servers. The extension runs entirely in your browser.

## What the extension stores
- **Your settings only.** Two on/off preferences (blocking enabled, and whether
  Shorts open as normal videos) are saved using Chrome's built-in
  `chrome.storage.sync` / `storage` API. This stays in your browser (and, if you
  are signed in to Chrome, syncs to your own Google account via Chrome — it is
  never sent to us).

## What the extension accesses
- **YouTube pages only.** The extension is granted access to `*.youtube.com` so
  it can detect Shorts URLs, redirect them, and hide Shorts sections. It does not
  read, collect, or send the content of any page.

## What the extension does NOT do
- No personally identifiable information, browsing history, or usage data is
  collected.
- No data is shared with or sold to third parties.
- No remote code is loaded or executed.

## Permissions
- `storage` — save your on/off settings locally.
- Host access to `*://*.youtube.com/*` — run on YouTube to block/hide Shorts.
